Test assertions no longer fail on text that is present: `assert_contains` used `echo "$haystack" | grep -qF`, and because `grep -q` exits at the first match, a haystack larger than the pipe buffer left `echo` writing into a closed pipe — which `set -o pipefail` turned into a failed assertion on Linux only. Pinned the `/finish` docs-rule test to `LC_ALL=C sort` so its expected output does not depend on the runner locale.
