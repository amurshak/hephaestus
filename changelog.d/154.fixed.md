**Hermes delegation now actually happens** (#154): measured against a credentialed Hermes v0.15.1 install (`anthropic/claude-opus-4.6` via the nous provider), the orchestrator never called `delegate_task` at all — it silently inlined every role the workflow named. Nine live `/refactor` runs against fixture repos settle what #153 left open. Delegation was not broken: told explicitly to delegate, the orchestrator batched six coder delegates that each rewrote their own module correctly, so the `context` wording #153 landed is already sufficient — every child received an absolute path in its goal, an absolute `cd` in its verify command, and resolved both without the parent conversation. What failed was reach: the skills described delegation rather than requiring it, and both delegation call sites in the workflow bodies are conditional ("For large refactors spanning multiple modules…", "For independent refactoring tasks across different files…"), leaving the orchestrator free to judge the condition unmet and do the work itself. It always did. The fix is wording in `scripts/sync-hermes-adapters.sh` and needs both halves: `render_hermes_skill`'s callout now states that a step naming a role **must** be delegated, and `hermes_localize_body` rewrites the conditional call sites into imperatives. Each half alone changes nothing — measured 0/1 for the callout alone and 0/1 for the call sites alone, against 0/1 for the original wording and 4/4 with both, all on the same fixture and prompt. Scope: one model, one workflow, six-module fixtures; the counts are small and delegation is stochastic, so treat 4/4 as a strong signal rather than a guarantee.
