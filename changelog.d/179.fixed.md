`/finish` now splits branch cleanup by checkout context. Inside a linked worktree it no longer checks out the base branch, deletes its own branch, or pops the shared stash — all three fail or misfire there, and `git worktree remove .` succeeds by deleting the session's own cwd mid-run. Worktree sessions close the issue, sweep remote branches, and report, then stop; `/orient` chains `/worktrees cleanup` in the primary checkout to reap what they left.
