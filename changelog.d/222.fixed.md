Unmet requirements and exhausted gates no longer fall through to shipping (#222). A blocked task that a required acceptance criterion depends on now winds down `[WIP]` instead of being skipped with a TODO; only optional work is deferred. The tester reports BLOCKED when a check cannot run, and `/test-issue` reports each criterion as met, unmet, or unverified with an overall outcome. `/ship` turns a failed lint, test, or build gate, an unavailable gate, or a still-failing review into a draft PR that ends the run without auto-merge, and `/autopilot` proceeds to `/ship` only on a `ready` outcome — including when incomplete work has no draft PR yet, and `/finish` reports a draft as not shipped rather than awaiting merge. The escalation spec now says required criteria and gates never degrade gracefully.
