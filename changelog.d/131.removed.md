**Git submodule distribution** (#131): `install.sh` no longer adds hephaestus as a `.hephaestus` submodule, and nothing symlinks through it. The pattern was broken in the environment hephaestus creates most often — `git worktree add` does not populate submodules, so every symlink in `.claude/`, `.opencode/`, `.agents/`, `.codex/`, and `.hermes/` dangled inside a worktree, exactly where coder agents run. Its one unique benefit, a version pin in the consuming repo, is now served by `install.sh --vendor` with none of the machinery: no `.gitmodules`, no detached HEAD, no `--recurse-submodules` in CI. Distribution tooling drops from 780 lines to 560 while gaining a mode.
