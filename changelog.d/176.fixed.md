`check_conventions.sh` gives the retry-limit restatement a shape instead of parsing free prose. A count binds to one of the spec's own nouns (`iterations`/`cycles`); a count on a generic noun ("max 2 retries") is reported as unrestated rather than guessed at, and limit vocabulary in any other shape ("max four iterations", "a 4-iteration maximum", "iterations: 4") fails loudly instead of slipping through unchecked. The shape check needs a spec noun within four words of the quantifier and reads prose only, so an unrelated cap, a word merely containing a loop noun, a quoted example, and a code-span identifier all stay silent.
