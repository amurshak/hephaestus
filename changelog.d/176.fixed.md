`check_conventions.sh` gives the retry-limit restatement a shape instead of parsing free prose. A count now binds to one of the spec's own nouns (`iterations`/`cycles`); a count on a generic noun ("max 2 retries") is reported as unrestated rather than guessed at, and limit vocabulary in any other shape ("max four iterations", "max 4 passes", "a 4-iteration maximum", "iterations: 4") fails loudly instead of slipping through unchecked. Caps that bound no loop, and identifiers in code spans, still read as prose.
