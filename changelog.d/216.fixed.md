**`collect-changelog.sh` fails loudly where it used to lose content silently** (#216): the fragment path was hardened by #205, #209, and #206, but the hand-written `## Unreleased` body and the document-structure paths had no equivalent guards, and five defects lived there. Unreleased content above the first `### ` heading — a bullet *or* a lead paragraph — was never rendered, yet the release blanked the whole body regardless, so it was deleted unpublished while the run printed `✓ released`; the release now refuses and quotes the orphaned lines, and preview warns about them alongside the other rehearsed preconditions (#214). A fragment body containing a `## ` line spliced in verbatim and forged a second version section, which also poisoned the next release because the duplicate-version guard greps for exactly that heading shape — now rejected in validation, with `### ` and deeper still allowed. Two `## Unreleased` sections published every entry twice and left both headings behind — now refused. A fold that renders nothing no longer publishes an empty section over content it is about to delete. And a fragment authored with its own leading `- ` renders one bullet instead of `- - entry`. Every case is covered in `tests/test_changelog_fragments.sh`, translated from the probes that found them.
