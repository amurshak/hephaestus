**OpenCode descriptions truncate on a word boundary, identically in every locale** (#137): `sync-opencode-adapters.sh` cut with a raw `${desc:0:180}`, so routing descriptions ended mid-word (`"…and spawn a seeded O"`, `"…an engineering gate and a gener"`) — and since bash substring expansion counts characters under a UTF-8 locale but bytes under `C`/`POSIX`, regenerating under a different `$LANG` produced different files and a spurious `--check` failure. The generator now uses the same `LC_ALL=C` word-whole trim the Hermes generator already had, so words are dropped whole, a multibyte character can never be split, and output is byte-identical under `C`, `POSIX`, and UTF-8 locales. Note the issue's suggested `export LC_ALL=C.UTF-8` is not portable — macOS ships no `C.UTF-8` locale.
