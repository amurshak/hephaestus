# General Critique Checklist

## Evaluate

- Logic: Does the conclusion follow from premises?
- Assumptions: What must be true for this to work?
- Completeness: What alternatives are missing?
- Trade-offs: What costs are hidden?
- Evidence: Is support empirical or speculative?
- Second-order effects: What downstream issues may appear?
- Timing: Is this decision right now?

## Verdict Criteria

- `SOUND`: reasoning is coherent and risks are acceptable.
- `NEEDS REFINEMENT`: viable, but gaps require changes.
- `RETHINK`: core assumptions or logic are unsound.
